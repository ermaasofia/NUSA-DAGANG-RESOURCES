About NusaDagang

Redefining the
Culinary Supply Chain

Nusa Dagang Resources specialises in high-quality, preservative-free food ingredients — from precision-blended spice pastes to chopped aromatics. We streamline kitchen operations for the F&B industry, central kitchens, and catering businesses, reducing prep time and labour costs without compromise.

Mission

To provide high-quality, preservative-free food processing solutions that enhance efficiency and consistency for businesses in the food industry.

Vision

To become the leading and most trusted food processing partner, delivering innovative solutions that support the growth of the global food service industry.
